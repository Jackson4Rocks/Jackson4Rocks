03 · About me

More developer,
less job title.

I like understanding how software meets hardware — and I tend to learn fastest by building something real enough to break.

Hi, I'm Leon.

I'm an Android developer with a strong interest in systems and open source. Most of my time goes toward Android apps, Android TV ideas, platform experiments, and learning how the layers underneath the interface fit together.

I've also spent time building Linux projects and experimenting with ROM/device work. That's part of the toolbox, not the identity: I'm much more interested in Android development as a whole than in calling myself a ROM builder.

The best projects are the ones where I can go from idea → code → build → boot → “why did that happen?” → fix.
